# Research Thesis

## Research question

How can an AI system adapt to a changing individual while remaining evidence-calibrated, correctable, computationally efficient, and supportive of human autonomy?

## Primary testable thesis

Structured, evidence-linked and revisable human modelling combined with selective cognition should improve adaptation over conversation-history-only personalization, without requiring every cognitive component to run on every interaction.

This thesis has not been proven. It requires operational definitions, comparative baselines, longitudinal evaluation, failure analysis, and human-subject safeguards before strong conclusions are justified.

## Proposed evaluation direction

Future experiments should compare the proposed approach with conversation-history-only personalization under matched tasks and resource constraints. Evaluation should measure predictive calibration, correction after disconfirming evidence, latency, compute use, user override success, and effects on human agency. No single score should stand in for the full research question.
